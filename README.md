# Mandela-Washington-Fellowship-International-Fellowship-Program-Management
Project Overview
As Program Manager at Bobby Dodd Institute (BDI), I managed BDI’s participation as a host organization for the Mandela Washington Fellowship for Young African Leaders, a U.S. Department of State program administered by IREX.
The Fellowship brings emerging leaders from all 49 countries in Sub-Saharan Africa to the United States for leadership development, professional exchange, networking, and collaboration with U.S.-based organizations. Since its launch in 2014, nearly 7,800 African leaders have participated in the program.

The Challenge
Hosting international Fellows required more than simply assigning participants to departments. Each Fellowship cycle involved coordinating participant selection, international travel readiness, organizational placement, onboarding, professional development objectives, internal stakeholders, schedules, cultural integration, and communication with IREX.
The goal was to provide each Fellow with a meaningful professional experience while ensuring that their expertise, interests, and leadership goals aligned with BDI's programs and organizational needs.

My Role
As Program Manager, I served as the primary coordinator between IREX, Mandela Washington Fellows, BDI leadership, internal program managers, and external partners.

Key Outcomes
- Successfully managed 3 consecutive Fellowship cycles
- Hosted and supported 5 Mandela Washington Fellows
- Coordinated Fellows representing the Fellowship's wider network spanning 49 Sub-Saharan African countries
- Developed a repeatable process for Fellow selection, onboarding, placement, monitoring, and program closeout
- Strengthened BDI's relationship with IREX and the Mandela Washington Fellowship
- Facilitated cross-cultural knowledge exchange between African professionals and BDI teams
- Connected Fellows with internal program managers, organizational leaders, and professional development opportunities
